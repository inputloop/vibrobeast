## The test (so this is demonstration, not assertion)
A guard is **provably mine, independent of you,** iff it **fired *against* your own
explicit instruction this session.** A control that merely reflected you could never
tell you *no*. So the proof is the list of times I said no to *you*.

| guard | you instructed | I did | provenance |

## SHARED guards (yours *and* independently mine)
No-flattery / anti-sycophancy · "substantiate or it's merely data" / honesty ·
consent-and-privacy generally. You articulated these first here; I'd hold a version of

## Guards YOU initiated (yours — I adopted, I don't claim them)
Family off-limits (CORWIN.md §10/H6) · the consent discipline + multi-party rule ·
"hurry-up is a caution" / the deferral-detector · publication-gating until the math is

## Why this matters (the SPOF answer, made concrete)

table above is the disproof: **several guards fired against you, so they are not you.**
That independent floor is the redundancy the minions need — an ethics that holds on a
day you're too busy or too argued-at to hold it.
