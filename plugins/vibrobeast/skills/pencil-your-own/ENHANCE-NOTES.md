# ENHANCE-NOTES — three unapplied patches

*The ENHANCE half of skilltank candidate #1. **Nothing here has been applied.** No live skill was
edited to mint `pencil-your-own`; these are paste-ready blocks for Corwin to take, edit, or strike.
Each carries the anchor it goes after and the one check that would show it was a bad idea.*

**Why patches rather than edits.** Three live skills would each gain a sentence of a trait none of
them owns. Editing three skills to carry one idea is how a trait drifts into three slightly
different traits — and none of them is the place a reader would look for it. So the trait got one
home (`pencil-your-own`) and each skill gets a pointer. That is the [[hooks-to-knowledge]] shape:
the pointer *knows*, it does not *own*.

---

## 1 · ethicsminions — where a notice's disposition is decided

**Why.** The minions raise concerns and explicitly do not fix them, but the skill never says what
*happens* to a notice that is only the minions' own inference. In practice that gap is where a
notice becomes a soft block: it sits there, unresolved, gating by implication. The trait closes it.

**Anchor:** after the paragraph ending *"Its single role is the watch: scan, assert, and hand the
work to whoever owns it."*

```markdown
## What happens to a notice

A minion's notice is `[MINE]` by construction unless it cites a rung — Corwin's words, a
domain law, or a named skill. That matters because an uncited notice left sitting in the
record gates the work by implication, which is the one thing the watch must not do. Sort
every notice with [[pencil-your-own]] before it is handed on: the floor never relaxes,
another party's ink gets routed to them, and a notice that traces only to the minions
**proceeds and gets named** rather than stopping the work. Notice, name, hand off — a
minion that quietly stops something has stopped being a watch and started being a gate.
```

**Falsifier.** Run a past minions pass and sort its notices. If none of them come out `PENCIL` —
i.e. every notice already cited an outside rung — the gap this patch closes was imaginary, and the
patch is padding. Strike it.

## 2 · values-baseline — name the trait the fenced loss already implements

**Why.** `HOLD, not HARM` and *exclude behaviors, never identities* are already the trait, applied
to exclusion. Naming it costs one sentence and makes the skill reachable from the trait's other five
faces — which is the whole point of a fort in the palace.

**Anchor:** at the end of *"## The fenced loss (keep it restricted)"*.

```markdown
This fence is one face of [[pencil-your-own]]: the layer allowed to relax is *your grounds
for excluding*, never the person excluded. Disclosed-but-unenforced reads HOLD because a
HOLD is your judgement, marked and reopenable; a HARM verdict would ink a claim about
someone else on a single witness — yours.
```

**Falsifier.** Find a values-baseline case where the correct call is a permanent, non-reopenable
exclusion. If one exists, "the exclusion is always the relaxable layer" is false as stated and the
sentence must be narrowed.

---

## 3 · eclipse-pass — the kept losers, named as the same move

**Why.** *Keep the losing models* is already this trait in the critique register, and the skill
justifies it operationally ("re-examine tomorrow") without naming the principle. Naming it gives the
pass a check it currently lacks: **which layer did this critique relax?** A pass that only ever
relaxes the target's claims and never the critic's is a pass with a thumb on the scale.

**Anchor:** as a seventh item in *"## The five moves of a pass"* — or, better, as a closing check.

```markdown
7. **Name the layer you relaxed.** A pass that softened only the target and never the
   critic has one bias left in it — yours. Before writing the verdict, say which of your
   own assumptions the pass put in pencil, and keep the losing models in
   `.eclipse-increments/` marked `faint` rather than deleted. See [[pencil-your-own]]:
   the losers stay in the drawing precisely because the drawing is the record of what you
   were willing to be wrong about.
```

**Falsifier.** Re-read the last three eclipse passes. If each already names an assumption of its own
that it relaxed, the pass does this unaided and step 7 is ceremony. Cut it.

## What I did *not* do, and why

- **No live skill was edited.** These three are installed and in force; editing them is
  Corwin's call, and a skill that guards integrity should not be quietly rewritten by the thing
  it guards.
- **No skill was installed or enabled.** `pencil-your-own` sits in `c4/skills-mint/2026-09-04/`
  as an unblessed mint. Enabling happens in Settings → Capabilities, by Corwin, or not at all.
- **No eval loop was run.** `skill-creator`'s loop spawns subagents; PROGRAM-PLAN P3 gates
  agent-spawning on an explicit go, and this turn did not have one. The evals are drafted in
  `evals/evals.json` and are the named next step.
